STAGE 02 • PAGE 2

Extruder, Hot Die Cutter & Pellet Dryer

  • • Barrel zone heaters (85–110°C) & melt pressure sensor
  • • Pelletizer rotary knife clearance & water-ring circulation
  • • Dewatering vibrating screen springs & cyclone blower cleanliness
  • • Gearbox oil sight glass level & cooling coil temperature
STAGE 03 • PAGE 3

Rotary EVA Injection Molding (IMM 01–06)

  • • Mold heating platen uniformity (165–175°C) & insulation
  • • Clamping hydraulic pressure (140–160 bar) & oil temp (<55°C)
  • • Vacuum pump suction gauge (-0.08 MPa) & receiver drain
  • • Safety light curtain & rotary table index locking pin
STAGE 04 • PAGE 4

Process Chillers, Air Compressors & 5S

  • • Chilled water supply temp (12–16°C) & return strainer check
  • • Compressed air FRL auto-drain, oil mist lubricator & pressure
  • • Mold release agent spray booth exhaust & floor slip prevention
  • • Shadow board tool verification & EVA scrap bin segregation
